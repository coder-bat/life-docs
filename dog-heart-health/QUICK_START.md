# Quick Start Guide - Dog Heart Health Website

## How to Access the Website

1. **Local Viewing**: Simply open `index.html` in any web browser
2. **No Installation Required**: The website is pure HTML/CSS with no dependencies

## Navigation

The website features a **sticky navigation bar** at the top with quick links to all sections:
- Overview
- Nutrition
- Exercise
- Supplements
- Prevention
- Warning Signs
- At-Risk Breeds
- References

Click any link to jump directly to that section.

## Key Sections to Explore

### For All Dog Owners:
- **Warning Signs of Heart Disease** - Learn what symptoms require immediate veterinary attention
- **Preventive Care & Early Detection** - Understand screening schedules and tests
- **Nutrition** - Evidence-based dietary recommendations (including FDA grain-free diet findings)

### For Specific Breeds:
- **Breeds at Higher Risk** - Find your dog's breed and learn specific risks and screening recommendations
  - Small breeds (Cavalier King Charles Spaniels, Dachshunds, Chihuahuas, etc.)
  - Large breeds (Doberman Pinschers, Boxers, Great Danes, Golden Retrievers, etc.)

### For Dogs with Heart Disease:
- **Evidence-Based Supplements** - Research-backed supplements with dosages
- **Exercise Guidelines** - Safe activity levels for dogs with cardiac conditions
- **Home Monitoring** - How to measure resting respiratory rate

## What Makes This Resource Reliable

✅ **8 Peer-Reviewed Studies** cited from leading veterinary journals

✅ **ACVIM Consensus Guidelines** for diagnosis and treatment
