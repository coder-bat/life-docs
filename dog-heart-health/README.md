This static website provides comprehensive, fact-checked information about improving canine heart health. All content is based on peer-reviewed veterinary research, clinical guidelines from international and Australian veterinary cardiology organizations, FDA investigations, and Australian Veterinary Association guidelines.

## Key Research Findings Highlighted

1. **Diet-Associated DCM**: FDA investigation (2018-2022) finding correlation between grain-free diets high in peas, lentils, and potatoes with DCM in dogs

2. **Taurine Deficiency**: Research by Kaplan et al. (2018) demonstrating taurine deficiency and DCM reversal in Golden Retrievers (75-90% improve with supplementation)

3. **EPIC Study**: Boswood et al. (2016) showing pimobendan extends time to heart failure by 15 months in dogs with Stage B2 MVD

4. **Breed-Specific Prevalence**: Detailed statistics including Cavaliers (100% MVD by age 10), Dobermans (50-60% DCM by age 8-10, 30% sudden death), Boxers (30-40% ARVC)

5. **Heart Rate Norms**: Comprehensive reference ranges by size and breed for baseline monitoring

6. **Australian Heartworm**: Endemic status with 10-30% infection rate in unprotected dogs in high-risk regions; year-round prevention mandatory

7. **Cardiac Biomarkers**: NT-proBNP reference ranges for screening (<900 pmol/L normal, >1,800 pmol/L indicates disease)

8. **Climate Impact**: Australian heat stress (35-45°C) significantly impacts cardiac patients; exercise timing critical
