# Dog Heart Health - Evidence-Based Resource

## Overview

This static website provides comprehensive, fact-checked information about improving canine heart health. All content is based on peer-reviewed veterinary research, clinical guidelines from veterinary cardiology organizations, and FDA investigations.

## Key Research Findings Highlighted

1. **Diet-Associated DCM**: FDA investigation (2018-2022) finding correlation between grain-free diets high in peas, lentils, and potatoes with DCM in dogs

2. **Taurine Deficiency**: Research by Kaplan et al. (2018) demonstrating taurine deficiency and DCM reversal in Golden Retrievers

3. **EPIC Study**: Boswood et al. (2016) showing benefits of early intervention in dogs with preclinical heart disease

4. **Omega-3 Benefits**: Multiple studies showing cardiovascular benefits of EPA and DHA supplementation
